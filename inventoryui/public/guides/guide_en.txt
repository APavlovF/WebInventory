QUICK-START GUIDE

Phase 1: Initial Setup
Complete these two steps first to configure your system:

1. Add Raw Materials (Market -> Buy materials)
   Enter your list of available raw materials and initial quantities into the system.

2. Define Products (Workshop -> Add product type)
   Create your product catalog and set up their Bills of Materials (recipes) to define how much raw material each product uses.

(Your initial setup is now complete.)

---

Phase 2: Daily Operations & Management
Once set up, manage your ongoing workflow using these actions:

- Manufacture Goods (Workshop -> Produce batch)
  Select a product and enter the quantity to produce. The system will automatically verify if you have enough raw materials and alert you if stock is too low.

- Record Sales (Market -> Sell products)
  Log outgoing orders to deduct finished products from your stock.

- Configure Alerts (Warehouse -> Setup stock limits)
  Set minimum and maximum thresholds to receive low-stock or overstock alerts.

- Archive Items (Office -> Remove material / Remove product)
  Discontinue raw materials or products you no longer use.
